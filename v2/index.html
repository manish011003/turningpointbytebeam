Limitations of this prototype
  • Every vehicle, workshop, quote and price here is invented. Nothing is a market valuation.
  • Cash purchase only. Finance comparison is not implemented, to avoid misleading loan calculations.
  • Totals are net cash outflow over the chosen period, in INR. Each path counts either sale or scrap proceeds, never both.
  • Running costs exclude repairs, depreciation and purchase costs, so nothing is counted twice.
  • Missing values are shown as missing and never treated as zero.
  • Quote extraction is simulated with fixed demo fields. No file is uploaded or read, and no model is called.
  • Ranges are scenario ranges built from the low and high values entered. They are not confidence intervals, probabilities or forecasts.
  • Turning Point does not certify roadworthiness, decide eligibility, predict sale prices, or carry out any sale, purchase or scrapping. It is a comparison tool, not advice.
